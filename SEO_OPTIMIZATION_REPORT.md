## Performance Metrics to Track

### Google Search Console (Weekly)
- **Impressions:** How many times site appears in search
- **Clicks:** How many people click through
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Clicks/Impressions (aim for 3-5%)
- **Average Position:** Where you rank (lower is better)

### Google Analytics (Daily/Weekly)
- **Users:** Total visitors
- **Sessions:** Number of visits
- **Bounce Rate:** % who leave immediately (aim for <50%)
- **Session Duration:** How long people stay (aim for 2+ min)
- **Pages per Session:** How many pages viewed (aim for 2+)
